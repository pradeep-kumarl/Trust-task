🚨 Scam Blacklist & Red Flag Guide

Learn how to instantly spot fake survey portals, WhatsApp task traps, and advance-fee schemes.

Advance Fee / "Activation" Trap 100% Scam

Any site or WhatsApp recruiter that asks you to pay a "registration fee", "ID verification charge", or "starter kit fee" is a scam. Legitimate platforms pay YOU; you never pay them.

Telegram / WhatsApp Task Scams High Danger

"Like YouTube videos or rate Google Maps places for ₹5,000/day." They pay ₹150 for the first task, then ask you to deposit money into a "prepaid trading task" to withdraw. Your deposit is stolen.

Astronomical Payout Promises Phishing Trap

"Complete a 5-minute survey and get a $500 gift card." Real surveys pay $1 to $5. Ridiculous promises exist only to harvest your email, credit card details, or install malware.

$100+ Minimum Cashout Trap Payment Trap

Sites requiring $100 or $150 minimum withdrawal. You earn quickly up to $92, then survey invitations stop completely or your account is banned right before reaching threshold.

PDF Retyping & Data Entry Fraud Freelance Scam

"Retype scanned image/PDF pages to MS Word for $30/page ($1,200 total)." After submitting work, scammers demand a "security deposit" or "notary license fee" before sending payment. Genuine clients never charge freelancers.

Fake Check / Equipment Purchase Scheme Check Fraud

A "remote employer" sends a check for $2,500 to buy home-office hardware from their "vendor". The check initially clears, but bounces weeks later. You lose the money sent to the fake vendor.
